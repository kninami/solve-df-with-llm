# Petition the metaverse service provider for enhanced logs and corroborate with circumstantial artifacts

## Summary

Since detailed avatar motion and voice-conversation logs are not currently generated by metaverse platforms, build the strongest available case from the circumstantial artifacts the five-area ecosystem process does recover (shared world membership and timing, paired-device identity, linked-messenger contact), and use that evidence to support a legal warrant requesting the metaverse service provider's own server-side logs.

## Addresses

- [[weaknesses/Metaverse platforms do not log avatar motion or voice conversation content]]

## How To Apply

Document the coarse but real evidence available (which world was joined, when, by which confirmed device/account pair, and any linked-messenger contact) as the basis for a legal request to the platform operator for any server-side logs they may retain beyond what is available on the client/HMD. Where the investigation is part of a broader effort, advocate (as a matter of platform social responsibility) for metaverse vendors to enhance their own local-device and server-side logging of in-world behavioral data, since local-device artifacts alone are unlikely to ever capture voice content or fine-grained motion.
