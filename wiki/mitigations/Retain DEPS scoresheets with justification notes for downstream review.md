# Retain DEPS scoresheets with justification notes for downstream review

## Summary

Require first responders to attach a brief justification note to each DEPS scoresheet answer and retain the completed scoresheet as a permanent case record, so a downstream examiner or reviewer can evaluate, and if necessary challenge, the reasoning behind a device's assigned priority rather than accepting the score alone at face value.

## Addresses

- [[weaknesses/First responders can inflate device-prioritisation scoresheet answers to increase seizure priority]]

## How To Apply

Build the justification requirement into the scoresheet template itself, so a first responder cannot submit a high-relevance answer without recording the specific observation supporting it (e.g., "device found in suspect's hand," "device screen displayed relevant chat content"). Have a supervising officer or the receiving examiner periodically spot-check completed scoresheets against the recorded justification, flagging entries where the justification does not plausibly support the score given. Treat a documented pattern of unsupported high scores from a given responder or unit as an indicator warranting additional training or review, rather than adjusting individual scores retroactively without evidence.
