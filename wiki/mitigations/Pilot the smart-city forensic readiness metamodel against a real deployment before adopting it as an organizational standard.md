# Pilot the smart-city forensic readiness metamodel against a real deployment before adopting it as an organizational standard

## Summary

Before adopting a literature-derived forensic readiness metamodel such as HADFRM as a standing organizational standard, pilot it against a real smart-city or smart-home deployment or a past real incident's data, and track any concepts, attributes, or relationships that had to be added or reinterpreted during the pilot.

## Addresses

- [[weaknesses/A unified smart-city forensic readiness metamodel has not been validated against a real-world deployment or incident]]

## How To Apply

Run the metamodel's vertical-transformation process (instantiating M1/M0 models from the M2 metamodel) against a genuine incident case file or a live smart-city testbed rather than only a constructed example, and feed back any gaps found into the metamodel's concept and process definitions before relying on it operationally.
