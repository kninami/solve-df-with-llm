# Corroborate device-configuration indicators of coercive control with case context before concluding abuse

## Summary

Treat a restrictive or monitoring-capable device setting as a possible investigative lead requiring further scrutiny, not as standalone proof of abuse, and evaluate each finding against surrounding case circumstances and testimony before concluding it reflects coercive control.

## Addresses

- [[weaknesses/Device settings indicating parental-control-like restrictions can be misinterpreted as evidence of coercive control]]

## How To Apply

For each identified setting (e.g. an active Screen Time passcode controlled by another party, or Family Sharing location sharing with an unfamiliar account), determine who controls the setting, whether the alleged victim was aware of or consented to its configuration, and whether an innocent explanation (parental control of a minor's own device, self-imposed productivity limits) is plausible given the case context. Corroborate with testimony from the alleged victim and other available evidence before characterizing a setting as evidence of DCC, and document the setting's known legitimate uses alongside its potential abusive use when reporting findings.
