# Treat DFRCF-DFMM maturity scores as a starting conversation, not a validated benchmark, until further field testing accumulates

## Summary

Use the extended DFRCF/DFMM maturity assessment as a structured starting point for an organization's internal forensic-readiness conversation and planning, rather than as a validated external benchmark to be compared across organizations, given the model's limited (10-practitioner) validation basis.

## Addresses

- [[weaknesses/DFR maturity assessment model structure was validated with only 10 practitioners and lacks practical field testing]]

## How To Apply

When applying the model, supplement the checklist/narrative assessment with organization-specific clarification of DFR-related roles and an explicit review of the privacy implications of any private information the assessment process itself collects, since the published model does not resolve these points. Avoid presenting the resulting maturity score as a validated, cross-organization comparable metric until further field-tested evidence accumulates.
