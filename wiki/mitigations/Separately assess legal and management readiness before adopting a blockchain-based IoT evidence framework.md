# Separately assess legal and management readiness before adopting a blockchain-based IoT evidence framework

## Summary

Before adopting a blockchain-based IoT evidence-management framework for real casework, conduct a separate legal/regulatory admissibility review and organizational-management readiness assessment, since these factors are typically underdeveloped in the published technical literature relative to integrity, storage, authentication, transparency, and security.

## Addresses

- [[weaknesses/Blockchain-based IoT evidence management frameworks largely neglect legal and management readiness]]

## How To Apply

Engage legal counsel to confirm that blockchain-recorded evidence and custody records would be admissible under the relevant jurisdiction's rules of evidence and any applicable data-protection regulations, since a framework's technical soundness does not establish its legal standing. Separately, assess organizational readiness — whether staff are trained to operate the framework correctly, whether leadership supports the process changes it requires, and whether supporting infrastructure and policy exist — before relying on it in an active investigation, rather than assuming published technical validation implies operational readiness.
