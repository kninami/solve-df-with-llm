# Establish a scheduled review process for domain-specific ontology coverage

## Summary

Treat a domain-specific cyber-threat ontology as requiring ongoing maintenance rather than a one-time deliverable; establish a recurring review process involving literature review and domain-expert engagement to identify and add coverage for newly emerged threats, device types, or attack techniques.

## Addresses

- [[weaknesses/Domain-specific cyber-threat ontologies require continuous manual updates to remain current]]

## How To Apply

Schedule periodic reviews (e.g., aligned with major security-industry threat reports or standards updates) of a domain-specific ontology's coverage against recent developments in the target domain, involving subject-matter experts where possible. When an investigation surfaces a threat, device type, or evidence category the ontology does not yet represent, treat this as a signal to update the ontology rather than working around the gap ad hoc, and version the ontology so investigators can identify which coverage snapshot informed a given case's analysis.
