# Reinforce the full multi-phase OSINT methodology through practical training and workflow tooling

## Summary

Address the gap between OSINT methodology training and practice by pairing classroom instruction with close supervised practical application of all six phases, and by using workflow tooling or checklists that make the strategy, planning, validation, analysis, and distribution/evaluation phases an explicit, hard-to-skip part of an investigator's process, not just collection.

## Addresses

- [[weaknesses/Practicing OSINT investigators execute only the collection phase and skip the methodology's other phases]]

## How To Apply

Structure practical OSINT training so trainees work closely with experienced supervisors during their placement, since supervisor guidance was identified as a key factor in effective methodology use. Provide or require use of a case-management template or checklist that requires an investigator to record strategy/planning notes, source-validation steps, an analysis summary, and a distribution/reporting artefact for each OSINT task, so that skipping directly from collection to conclusion becomes visibly incomplete rather than the unremarked default.
