# Supplement a cognitive map's technical guidance with follow-up investigative and legal or operational planning

## Summary

Treat a phenomenon-specific cognitive map as guidance for the technical crime-scene portion of an investigation only, and separately plan the follow-up investigative, legal, and operational measures needed once a suspect is identified, rather than assuming the map's coverage is complete end to end.

## Addresses

- [[weaknesses/Phenomenon-specific cognitive maps omit follow-up investigative measures and non-technical case context]]

## How To Apply

When using a cognitive map to plan a cybercrime investigation, explicitly hand off from the map's technical crime-scene guidance to a separate, standard operational checklist (arrest planning, prosecution-support evidence gathering, asset seizure, inter-agency coordination) once a suspect has been identified through the mapped measures. For operationally complex phenomena where a simple node-link view is insufficient, consider building a more granular, fully meshed interactive representation of the operational level rather than relying on the phenomenon-level map alone.
