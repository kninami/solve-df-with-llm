# Adopt a structured technology-method-application reliability-documentation framework as standard operating procedure

## Summary

Require every digital forensic report to document, at a minimum, the technology/tool used and its known reliability/error-rate status, the method applied and its accepted-methodology basis, and how that method was specifically configured and executed in the case at hand, as a standing organizational policy rather than leaving reliability documentation to individual examiner discretion.

## Addresses

- [[weaknesses/Digital forensic investigation reports frequently lack sufficient documentation to assess evidence reliability]]

## How To Apply

Adopt [[techniques/Document digital forensic reliability using a structured technology-method-application validation framework]] (or an equivalent structured framework) as mandatory standard operating procedure across an organization's digital forensic unit, rather than relying on individual examiners to document reliability information ad hoc. Build report templates that require the technology, method, and application levels to be explicitly completed before a report is finalized, and periodically audit a sample of completed reports (as this paper's own methodology did) against the framework's requirements to verify the policy is actually being followed in practice, not merely mandated on paper.
